Am I even mentioned in AI?

That question kept coming.

This is the story of how three friends built an answer to it.

01The question

It started with customers asking the same thing

Damian has run IQ Script, an agency for websites and findability, for eight years. In 2025 customers kept asking him the same question: "Am I even mentioned when someone asks ChatGPT?"

The honest answer was that nobody knew. So he set out to find out. He did a lot of research and tested what does and does not work to end up in AI answers. Much more turned out to be possible than he expected.

02The proof

Two carpenters in one week

The first time it really worked was at his own company. IQ Script published a few articles about carpenters.

Five to six weeks later, two requests came in within one week. Both from a carpenter, both from the same region. In eight years that had never just happened.

03The partnership

Three friends, one system

Damian told Tim. Together with Thimo, Tim builds AI software at The AI Doctor that helps companies work smarter. Those two have known each other for a very long time, and Damian knows them through a mutual friend.

The idea was simple: one knows what works to get found, the others know how to turn that into a system. In February 2026 we started building.

04The roles

All three of us build everything

Damian brings the knowledge of findability and designed the brand. Tim and Thimo are the technical side. Thimo also does sales.

Thimo has been active on LinkedIn for years and saw how much business companies get from it. That is why NXEO can also publish on LinkedIn, next to the content on your own website.

05The choice

Why it did not become an agency

We saw agencies trying this too. Often for a lot of money, with hours, separate campaigns and a dashboard you have to dig through yourself. We wanted it differently.

NXEO is one fixed amount per month. We do the work, you approve, and everything we make stays yours.

06The surprise

It also works for who you do not want to reach

Ferry of De Scooter Dokter comes by with his van and works on your scooter at your door. He got many requests from regions he does not serve at all. That was a big frustration for him.

Since his website clearly states where he does and does not work, he is contacted more often from his own region and less from outside it. Findability works both ways.

07Why this matters

You can exist for a hundred and fifty years and still not be mentioned

You can be the best carpentry firm or law firm in the region. If AI does not mention you when someone asks, you stay dependent on word of mouth. Nothing wrong with that, but it does not make you grow.

That is why we build NXEO for companies of 5 to 50 people that win customers on trust. First in the Netherlands, then in Belgium, Germany, Spain and English speaking Europe.
