Questions about NXEO and Webflow

Do I have to install or set up anything myself?

We set up the connection with your Webflow website together during onboarding. We walk you through what is needed at that point. You do not need technical knowledge for it.

Do I need a Webflow plan with CMS?

To place items in a collection your site needs a Webflow plan with CMS. During onboarding we look at what you have now and whether that is enough.

Does it go in my blog or as a separate page?

Both. An answer to one question fits as a CMS item in your blog or knowledge base. An explanation of a service or a region belongs on a page of its own.

Does anything go online without me knowing?

No. You see beforehand what is ready and you approve it. If you reject something, we replace it at no extra cost.

Can I edit the texts myself afterwards?

Yes. It is ordinary content on your own website. You edit it the way you are used to.

What happens to the content if I stop?

It stays. Everything we have created remains yours: the pages, the articles and your measurement data.
