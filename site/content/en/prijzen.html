Questions about pricing

Still have questions? Here it is, clearly.

Is there a free trial?

No. AI systems need weeks to pick up new content, so a trial month would show nothing yet. You do get a free baseline check first. Within 48 hours it shows where you stand today and which questions you lose, before you pay anything.

Why do I pay a €195 setup fee once?

It covers onboarding, the baseline and setup: we get to know your company, your tone and your customers, and prepare the connection to your website. We do that once, at the start.

Why is the minimum term 6 months?

Because visibility in AI takes time. You see the first results within 90 days, and it keeps building from there. With a shorter term you would stop before the work pays off. After 6 months you can cancel monthly.

How do I know it delivers enough?

You see it in black and white. The baseline shows where you start, and your report then shows which questions you gain. We do not promise a number of leads: we make sure you are mentioned for the questions your customers ask, and show that it happens.

Is the price per website?

Yes. A package is for one company with one website. If you have several brands, locations or countries, we make a custom proposal.

How and when do I pay?

Per month, or a year at once. If you pay a year upfront, you pay for 11 months instead of 12. All prices exclude VAT.

What happens if I stop?

Everything we made stays yours: the pages, the posts, the structure and your data. Nothing is removed.
