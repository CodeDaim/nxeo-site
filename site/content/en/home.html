Frequently asked questions

The questions we hear most

What exactly is an AI Presence score?

A score from 0 to 100 per channel (AI, Search, LinkedIn) and combined. For AI we ask ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity the questions your customers ask, every day, and count how often you are mentioned, in which position and whether the description is correct. For Search we look at Google AI Overviews and classic rankings. For LinkedIn at reach and reactions on your posts.

How quickly will I see results?

Within 48 hours the system runs and you have your baseline. After 30 days you know exactly where you stand. We usually see the first shifts in AI answers and LinkedIn reach between day 30 and 60, because AI systems need time to pick up new content. After 90 days your report shows in black and white what has changed.

Do I have to supply or write content myself?

No. We write everything: LinkedIn posts, blog articles, pages and FAQ blocks, based on the onboarding call, your existing material and what the data says AI is missing about you. You see posts in advance and approve them with one message. If you do want to give input (a case, an opinion, a photo), we work it in.

Am I locked in?

The first 6 months are the minimum term, after that you can cancel monthly. Pay a year at once and you pay for 11 months. The data, content and structure we build always stay yours.
