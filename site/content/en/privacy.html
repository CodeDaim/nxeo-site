NXEO respects the privacy of visitors to its website, and in particular their rights regarding the automated processing of personal data. In this privacy policy we explain which data we collect, why, and how you can exercise your rights.

Article 7, your rights

Under Article 13(2)(b) of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), everyone has the right to access, rectify or erase their personal data, to restrict processing, to object to processing and to data portability.

You can exercise these rights by contacting us at support@nxeo.io.

Each request must be accompanied by a copy of a valid proof of identity bearing your signature, and the address at which you can be reached. You will receive a response within 1 month of the request. Depending on the complexity and number of requests, this period may be extended by 2 months.

You may receive commercial offers from the operator. If you no longer wish to receive them, send an email to support@nxeo.io.

Article 10, retention period

The data collected by the operator is used and stored for the period determined by law.

Article 11, cookies

A cookie is a small text file placed on the hard drive of your device when you visit our website. When you visit our website, a banner appears with information about the use of cookies.

We only place cookies after you have actively given consent via the cookie banner. Your consent is valid for a period of twelve months. You can change your preference at any time via the cookie settings.

We use the following cookies on our website:

  • Functional cookies, such as session cookies to store your session information. These are always placed because they are necessary for the website to work.
  • Analytical cookies (Google Analytics), to understand how our website is used based on anonymous information about visitor numbers, popular pages and topics. We have enabled IP anonymisation so we cannot identify you personally. These cookies are only placed after you have given consent.

We do not use tracking cookies for advertising purposes.

For more information about using, managing and deleting cookies for each operating system, see: autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl/cookies.
