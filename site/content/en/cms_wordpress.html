Check your WordPress in ten minutes

Six things we regularly come across on WordPress websites. They are separate from NXEO. You can check them yourself today.

Settings › ReadingSearch engine visibility

Is the tick box switched on that asks search engines to skip your site? It is switched on during the build and sometimes never switched off again.

Settings › PermalinksAddresses in plain words

Choose "Post name". An address with a question mark and a number tells a system nothing about the content.

Security pluginWhom you keep out

Plugins that keep out unknown visitors also shut out the search services AI systems lean on.

Theme or page builderText that only loads after a click

Tabs and collapsible blocks that only fetch their text after a click are empty to a system.

Posts › CategoriesEmpty archive pages

Dozens of categories and tags with one post each produce thin pages. A handful of clear categories works better.

yourwebsite.com/wp-sitemap.xmlYour sitemap

WordPress creates an overview of your pages by itself. Open the address and see whether your main pages are in it.

Questions about NXEO and WordPress

Do I have to install anything myself?

We set up the connection with your WordPress website together during onboarding. We walk you through what is needed at that point. You do not need technical knowledge for it.

Do you change anything about my theme or plugins?

No. We publish content: posts and pages. Your theme, your plugins and your settings stay as they are.

Does it go in my blog or as a separate page?

Both. An answer to one question fits as a post in your blog or knowledge base. An explanation of a service or a region belongs on a page of its own.

Does anything go online without me knowing?

No. You see beforehand what is ready and you approve it. If you reject something, we replace it at no extra cost.

Can I edit the texts myself afterwards?

Yes. They are ordinary posts and pages in your WordPress. You open them in the editor you are used to.

What happens to the content if I stop?

It stays. Everything we have created remains yours: the pages, the posts and your measurement data.
