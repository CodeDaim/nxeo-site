Questions about NXEO and WordPress

Do I have to install anything myself?

We set up the connection with your WordPress website together during onboarding. We walk you through what is needed at that point. You do not need technical knowledge for it.

Do you change anything about my theme or plugins?

No. We publish content: posts and pages. Your theme, your plugins and your settings stay as they are.

Does it go in my blog or as a separate page?

Both. An answer to one question fits as a post in your blog or knowledge base. An explanation of a service or a region belongs on a page of its own.

Does anything go online without me knowing?

No. You see beforehand what is ready and you approve it. If you reject something, we replace it at no extra cost.

Can I edit the texts myself afterwards?

Yes. They are ordinary posts and pages in your WordPress. You open them in the editor you are used to.

What happens to the content if I stop?

It stays. Everything we have created remains yours: the pages, the posts and your measurement data.
