Where it often goes wrong with WordPress

Three things we regularly come across on WordPress websites. They are separate from NXEO, you can check them yourself today.

The tick box under Reading

Under Settings and then Reading there is a tick box that asks search engines to skip your site. It is switched on during the build and sometimes never switched off again.

Security that is too strict

Security plugins can keep out unknown visitors. In doing so you also shut out the search services AI systems lean on.

Text that only loads after a click

Some themes and builders only load text in tabs or collapsible blocks after a click. A system does not click and then sees an empty space.
