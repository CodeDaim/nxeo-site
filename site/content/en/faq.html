Frequently asked questions

Honest answers to what people ask us most. Is your question missing? Ask it by email

What exactly is an AI Presence score?

A score from 0 to 100 per channel (AI, Search, LinkedIn) and combined. For AI we ask ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity the questions your customers ask, every day, and count how often you are mentioned, in which position and whether the description is correct. For Search we look at Google AI Overviews and classic rankings. For LinkedIn at reach and reactions on your posts.

What is GEO and how does it differ from SEO?

SEO targets rankings in classic search results. GEO (generative engine optimisation) targets being mentioned in AI answers. That requires different things: clear, factual, well structured content an AI model can cite, consistency across your website, LinkedIn and other sources, and recent publications. NXEO does both, but steers on the AI answer as the outcome.

How is this different from a marketing agency?

An agency sells hours and separate campaigns, and usually you have to decide what needs to happen. NXEO is a fixed subscription with a fixed cycle: measure, decide what needs to change, execute, remeasure. You pay for the outcome of that cycle, not for time.

What exactly do you measure, and how?

During onboarding we draw up a list with you of 25 to 50 questions your customers would ask, for example "which provider in region X helps with Y". We ask those daily, automatically, to ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google. We record who is mentioned, in which order, with which description and which sources. We do the same for your competitors. You can adjust the question list at any time.

What if a competitor also uses NXEO?

That can happen. We then work for both with the same effort, but with your own question list, your own content and your own competitor analysis. In practice both parties win from those who do nothing; AI answers usually mention several names.

Does this replace my SEO?

No, it sits next to it. SEO is about your place in Google search results, we focus on being mentioned in AI answers. Much of what we do helps both: clear pages, answers to real questions and good structure. That is why we measure Google too.

Do I need an ad budget?

No. We do nothing with advertising. You only pay for your package and the one time setup fee.

Will NXEO get us to number one?

That is certainly possible, and it happens. But we do not promise it, and nobody can honestly promise it.

Who AI mentions depends on more than the content we create: how well known your company already is, what others write about you, your reviews, how strong your competitors are and how the AI systems themselves change. We do not control part of that.

What we do: improve everything we can influence, every week, and show you what it delivers.

So what do you promise?

Things we control ourselves:

  • A baseline within 48 hours, so you know where you start.
  • New content every week, written and published, according to your package.
  • A report showing which questions you are and are not mentioned for.
  • Everything we make stays yours, also if you stop.
Do you guarantee leads or revenue?

No. We make sure you are mentioned more often for the questions your customers ask. Whether someone then gets in touch and becomes a customer also depends on your offer, your price, your website and how fast you respond. We do not control that, so we give no guarantee on it.

Does "being number one" even exist in AI?

Not like in Google. An AI answer differs per person, per moment and per way of asking. One time you are at the top, another time third, sometimes not there.

That is why we do not measure one position, but how often you are mentioned across a whole list of questions, day after day. That is the number we want to raise.

What if I see little difference after 90 days?

Usually you see movement within 90 days. In a crowded market, or if your company has few traces online, it can take longer.

Either way you see it in black and white in your report. Then we discuss what to change: other questions, other topics or another approach. After the first 6 months you can cancel monthly.

Can you correct wrong information about me in AI?

Often, but not on command. We cannot edit an AI system directly. We can make sure the right information is clearly on your own website and channels, so AI picks it up. That usually takes a few weeks.

How quickly will I see results?

Within 48 hours the system runs and you have your baseline. After 30 days you know exactly where you stand. We usually see the first shifts in AI answers and LinkedIn reach between day 30 and 60, because AI systems need time to pick up new content. After 90 days your report shows in black and white what has changed.

Do I have to supply or write content myself?

No. We write everything: pages, blog articles, FAQ blocks and LinkedIn posts, based on the onboarding call, your existing material and what the data says AI is missing about you. You see posts in advance and approve them with one message. If you do want to give input (a case, an opinion, a photo), we work it in.

What does the weekly report look like?

One page. At the top your presence score per channel and the change from last week. Below that: which questions you won or lost this week, which competitor stands out, which posts did well, what we changed on your website and what we do next week. You get it Monday morning by email and it is always in your NXEO workspace.

How much of my time does this take per week?

Count on fifteen minutes: reading the report and approving the posts. In the first week we need one hour of your time for the onboarding call. After that you read the weekly report every Monday. Nothing more is needed.

Does this work in my industry?

It works for any B2B company whose customers research online. Niche markets in particular have a lot to gain, because AI systems often have no clear preference there yet. Not sure? Request a baseline measurement and see right away how your brand stands today.

A report on where you stand today. You enter your website and within 48 hours you receive by email: your Presence score, the questions you lose today, how AI describes your company and the first three steps we would take. It is free and there is no obligation.

Do I need a website?

Yes. We publish the content on your own website, so it needs to exist. We work with WordPress, Webflow, Framer, Wix and Shopify, among others. Building a new website is not part of the package.

What access do you need?

  • Admin role on your LinkedIn company page, so we can schedule posts.
  • Optional: access to Google Search Console and Analytics, so we can measure traffic from AI and Google.

Prefer that we publish nothing ourselves? Then we deliver everything and you place it.

Do you post on LinkedIn on my behalf?

By default we publish from your company page. If you also want to post from your personal profile (which works better for reach), we write the post and you place it yourself, or you give us access via LinkedIn's own scheduling feature. We never ask for your password and never publish anything without your approval.

What happens to my website?

We add content and adjust structure: new pages, FAQ blocks, structured data, internal links and improved copy. We do not change your design and remove nothing without consulting you. Every change is listed in the weekly report.

Can I reject things or steer?

Yes, always. Posts only go live after your approval. Topics you do not want go on a list. You can adjust the tone, the frequency and the question list at any time.
