Questions about a custom connection

Do I have to install or set up anything myself?

We set up the connection with your website together during onboarding. We walk you through what is needed at that point. You do not need technical knowledge for it.

My system was built in house or is a bit older. Can it be done?

In principle, yes. We look for a way that fits your system. If it turns out during onboarding that it cannot be done after all, we say so honestly before you pay anything.

Does it go in my blog or as a separate page?

Both. An answer to one question fits as an article in your blog or knowledge base. An explanation of a service or a region belongs on a page of its own.

Does anything go online without me knowing?

No. You see beforehand what is ready and you approve it. If you reject something, we replace it at no extra cost.

Can I edit the texts myself afterwards?

Yes. It is ordinary content on your own website. You edit it the way you are used to.

What happens to the content if I stop?

It stays. Everything we have created remains yours: the pages, the articles and your measurement data.
