"If AI does not mention your company, you do not exist." You read it in every presentation on this subject. It is a sentence meant to shake you awake, and like most such sentences it is exaggerated.

You exist. Your regular customers know where to find you. Your van drives through the neighbourhood, your name is on the front of the building and your neighbour recommends you.

Still, there is a core of truth in it that is worth taking seriously. In this article we strip off the exaggeration and look at what remains.

## Who the sentence does apply to

There is one group for whom you truly do not exist when AI does not mention you: people who do not know you yet and who let an AI assistant prepare their choice.

Follow one of them. She has a leaking roof window. She asks ChatGPT what the cause might be. She asks roughly what a repair costs. Then she asks who near Amersfoort can do that well. She gets three names with a sentence of explanation for each. She calls the first. They can come on Wednesday.

You were not among the three. You were not rejected, nor compared and found too expensive. You were never in view. For this customer, at this moment, you do not exist.

That is the difference with the past. In a list of ten search results you might have been in position six, and you still had a chance. In advice with three names there is no position six. How that advice comes about is covered in [how ChatGPT decides which companies to mention](#art_hoe_chatgpt_bepaalt_welke_bedrijven_het_noemt).

## Why you notice nothing

This is what makes it treacherous. If your website is down, you see it. If your phone does not work, you hear it. If AI does not mention you, nothing happens.

No alert arrives. Your visitor numbers do not fall dramatically. Your existing customers keep coming. There is no moment at which you think: now it is going wrong.

What happens is that part of the new enquiries ends up somewhere else. You do not see the customer you did not get. You only see, after a year or two, that a competitor has grown and you have stayed roughly where you were.

> You do not lose customers you had. You miss customers you never knew existed.

That also makes it hard to give it priority. There is always something that is on fire today.

## How much it costs you: three questions

Whether this is a large or a small problem for you depends on your company. Three questions take you a long way.

### 1. How many of your new customers look for information beforehand?

A bakery round the corner has little to do with this. People walk in. An installer of solar panels has everything to do with it. Nobody buys panels without asking questions first.

The larger the purchase and the more complicated the choice, the more is asked beforehand, and the greater the chance that this happens with an AI assistant.

### 2. How do your new customers reach you now?

If nine out of ten new customers come through an existing customer, you are less vulnerable. Although it pays to consider what that referred customer does before calling. Often they look you up. And more and more often they ask an AI assistant what is known about you.

If a large share comes in through searching, this shift affects you directly.

### 3. What is one new customer worth?

An accountant who keeps a client for eight years on average loses far more with one missed enquiry than the revenue of the first year. A painter with separate jobs loses one job, but also the neighbours who would have seen the van parked outside.

Put those three answers side by side. For many service providers the outcome is that a handful of missed enquiries per year is already a serious amount.

## The objection: my customers do not use that

We hear it often, and sometimes it is right. There are audiences that hardly use an AI assistant yet.

But be careful with that assumption, for two reasons.

The first is that people use it without calling it that. Anyone who types something into Google gets an AI Overview at the top more and more often. Anyone with an Android phone talks to Gemini. It sits inside the products they were already using.

The second is that use is broad. In 2025 OpenAI published a [study of how people use ChatGPT](https://openai.com/index/how-people-are-using-chatgpt/). Most use was private and not for work, and roughly half of the messages were requests for information or advice. Those are not programmers. Those are people with a question about their house, their health or their money.

If you want to be sure, ask. Put the question to your next ten new customers: how did you find us, and did you use an AI assistant? The answer surprises more often than you think.

## The reverse situation: being mentioned where you have no business

Not existing is one side. The other side is existing wrongly.

An AI assistant can mention you with information that is incorrect. A service you stopped offering. A service area larger than the real one. A price from four years ago. Then you get enquiries you can do nothing with, or worse: the customer drops out because the picture does not match what they see on your website.

For a system you exist the way it can read you. If what can be read about you is incomplete or outdated, so is your existence in AI. What to do when it goes wrong is covered in [AI says something wrong about my company](#art_ai_zegt_iets_verkeerds_over_mijn_bedrijf).

## What existing in AI means in concrete terms

Let us make it practical. You exist for an AI system when four things are true.

1. **It can find you.** Your website is reachable and known to the search services the systems use.
2. **It understands what you do.** Your website literally says what you deliver, for whom and where.
3. **It has something to quote.** There are answers to the questions customers ask.
4. **It trusts what it reads.** Other sources confirm your story and your details do not contradict each other.

If the first is missing, the other three do not help. If the third is missing, which is the case for most companies, you are found and understood and still not mentioned. You are there, but there is nothing to say about you.

Which of the four is missing for you can be worked out with [why am I not in AI recommendations](#art_waarom_sta_ik_niet_in_de_aanbevelingen_van_ai).

## Variations on the same sentence

The statement comes past in different forms. It pays to separate them, because they do not mean the same thing.

**"Whoever is not in AI does not exist."** Too strong. You exist for everyone who already knows you.

**"Whoever is not mentioned is not considered."** This is the version that is right. It is not about existing. It is about being included in the choice.

**"Second place in AI is the first loser."** Not true. An AI answer usually names several companies, and the order changes each time. What matters is that you are in the set.

**"In two years nobody will search on Google any more."** Not true. People use both. That is the subject of [is ChatGPT the Google of 2026](#art_is_chatgpt_de_google_van_2026).

**"You have to be in now, or you are too late."** Half true. There is no door that closes. It is the case, though, that whoever starts earlier builds a lead that someone else has to catch up. Content stacks.

## Why starting early does matter

That last point deserves explanation, because it is the only reason for haste that we believe in ourselves.

You build visibility in AI with content, and content adds up. Whoever answers a question from their customers every week for a year has fifty pages after that year. A competitor who starts then is at zero. They can catch up, but it costs them a year, and in that year you carry on.

On top of that, most companies have not done anything about this yet. In many regions and sectors there is nobody right now who answers customers' questions well. Whoever is there first has it to themselves for the time being.

We noticed ourselves how little is sometimes needed. A few articles about one trade on our own website produced two enquiries from that trade within one week, five to six weeks later. Not because the articles were so special. Because nobody else had written them.

## What you can do today

You do not need to panic and you do not need to buy anything. Start with knowing where you stand.

1. **Do the test.** Put ten customer questions to ChatGPT, Gemini and Claude, without naming your company. The whole method is in [is my company mentioned in AI](#art_mijn_bedrijf_genoemd_in_ai).
2. **See who is mentioned.** Open their website. What is there that is missing from yours?
3. **Write one answer.** Choose the question you get most often and put the answer on your website. Today.
4. **Ask your next customers how they found you.** Then in three months you know how big this is for you.

## Two companies, two years

A fictional comparison to show how the gap opens up. Two plumbing companies in the same town, the same size, with the same reputation.

The first answers one customer question on its website every week. What a new boiler costs. What to do when a radiator stays cold. Whether underfloor heating works with a wooden floor. Nothing brilliant, just the answers given on the phone every day.

The second does nothing. Business is fine, the phone rings, there is no reason to change anything.

After three months there is no visible difference. After a year the first company notices that new customers arrive better informed and mention what they read. After two years it is named for dozens of questions in its town, and the second company for none. The second company still has the same number of customers as before. It simply has none of the new ones.

Nothing dramatic happened to the second company at any point. That is exactly the problem.

## In summary

- You exist even when AI does not mention you. For new customers who choose through AI, you are simply not considered.
- You do not notice it through a decline, but through growth that fails to arrive.
- How heavily it weighs depends on how much your customers research beforehand and what a customer is worth to you.
- Existing in AI means: findable, understandable, quotable and credible.
- Starting early helps, because content adds up and most competitors have not started yet.
