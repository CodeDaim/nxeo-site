Copilot is Microsoft's AI system. It is in Windows, in the Edge browser and in Microsoft 365, so in Word, Outlook and Teams. Many people use it at work without ever consciously choosing it.

For a company that sells to other companies that is interesting. The buyer looking for a supplier already has Copilot open.

## Copilot searches through Bing

When Copilot looks something up on the internet, it uses Bing, Microsoft's search engine. So what Bing knows about you decides what Copilot can find.

This is a blind spot for many business owners. They know how they do in Google. Whether Bing knows their website, they have never checked.

## Check whether Bing knows you

That takes a few minutes.

1. Go to bing.com and search for `site:yourwebsite.com`. You see which pages Bing knows.
2. Then search for a question a customer would ask. Are you there?
3. Ask Copilot the same question and see which sources it names.

If you see little or nothing, Bing knows too little about your website.

## Submit your website to Bing

Microsoft has a free service, Bing Webmaster Tools. You can add your website and submit your sitemap. If you already have Google Search Console, you can import the settings from it, so you do not have to verify again.

After that you see which pages Bing has included and for which searches you appear.

## What else helps

The basis is the same as with the other systems:

- Say literally on your website what you do, for whom and where.
- Answer each customer question in its own place, with the answer at the top.
- Do not block search engines by accident.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do my customers even use Copilot?

That depends on your customers. If they work in an office with Microsoft 365, chances are Copilot is ready for them. With consumers it matters less.

### Is doing well in Google not enough?

No. Bing has its own index. It often overlaps, but not always. Checking takes a few minutes.

### Do I have to write different texts for Bing?

No. The same clear pages work in both. The difference is in submitting and checking.
