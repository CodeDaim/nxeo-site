What is NXEO? In one sentence: NXEO makes sure your company is mentioned when someone asks ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or Google a question to which you are the answer.

That is the short version. This article holds the long one: what exactly it does, what a week with NXEO looks like, what it costs and who it is not for. We wrote this ourselves, so we are not neutral. We do our best to be honest, including about what we cannot do.

## The problem NXEO solves

More and more people put their question to an AI assistant instead of a search engine. They do not get a list of ten links back, but an answer with two or three names. Whoever is not among them is not considered.

To be mentioned, your website needs clear and current content about the questions your customers ask. That is no secret. The problem is that the work is never finished. A page, an answer or an improvement every week. Most business owners know it has to happen and never get round to it.

There are two usual solutions. You do it yourself, which means it stalls after three weeks. Or you hire an agency, which quickly costs many times more. We felt something belonged in between. The trade off between those three routes is covered in [AI visibility: do it yourself, an agency or software](#art_ai_zichtbaarheid_zelf_doen_bureau_of_software).

## What NXEO does, in three steps

### 1. Measure

We draw up a list of the questions your customers ask AI. We put those questions to ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude and Perplexity every day, and we look at the AI answers and the regular results in Google.

For each question we see whether you are mentioned, in which place and whether the description is correct. We do the same for your competitors. That produces a score, the Presence score, and a list of questions someone else is winning right now.

### 2. Create

The list of missed questions decides what gets written. That can be a new page, an article, a block of frequently asked questions or an improvement to an existing text. It is written in your tone, based on an onboarding and on what is already known about your company.

In the plans with LinkedIn we also write posts. Website content is the core. LinkedIn comes on top.

### 3. Publish

This is the part where most tools stop, and the part we are about. The content is published in your own website. We work with WordPress, Wix and other widely used systems, among others.

You see in advance what is ready and you approve it. After that it is online. You do not need to log in, copy anything or call anyone.

## What a week with NXEO looks like

On Monday you receive an email with your report. It shows where you are mentioned now, what was published in the past week and what we will do in the coming week. With the entry plan you receive that report monthly.

In between you get a message when something is ready for approval. That is usually a matter of reading and agreeing. If you reject something, we replace it at no extra cost.

It asks no more than that. Clients tell us it takes them about a quarter of an hour per week.

**Within 48 hours** you have your baseline check. It shows where you stand now, which questions you are losing and how AI describes your company.

## What NXEO is not

This part is at least as important as the rest.

**NXEO is not a guarantee of position one.** There is no position one in an AI answer, and nobody can force what a system says. We make the odds as high as possible and show what happens.

**NXEO does not promise a number of leads.** We measure whether you are mentioned for the questions your customers ask. Whether an enquiry follows also depends on your offer, your price and your website. What you can do about that yourself is covered in [more leads from your website with better findability](#art_meer_leads_uit_je_website_met_betere_vindbaarheid).

**NXEO is not an agency.** You do not get an account team that comes to give a presentation every month. Much of the work is automated, and that is the reason the price is what it is. People work on it and you have one fixed contact, but we do not bill hours.

**NXEO does not build a new website.** We work in the website you have. Adverts, photography and video are not included either.

**NXEO is not a trial month.** Because results take weeks, you would see nothing in a trial month. That is why the baseline check beforehand is free. You then know where you start before you pay anything.

## Who it works for

NXEO was made for companies that depend on customers who have to find them, and that do not have a marketing department of their own.

Think of installers, builders, landscapers, accountants, lawyers, estate agents, practices, consultancies and companies that supply other companies. What they have in common: customers ask questions before they choose, and the answers to those questions can be explained.

It works best when:

- you have your own website on which we can publish content;
- your customers look for information beforehand;
- you are willing to look along for a quarter of an hour per week;
- you give it six months.

## Who it does not work for

- **You need customers tomorrow.** Then adverts are faster. NXEO builds something that lasts, and that takes time.
- **You have no website, or nothing may be changed on it.** Without a place to publish we cannot do anything.
- **Your customers do not search.** If you run entirely on a few regular clients and want to keep it that way, you do not need this.
- **You want to write every word yourself.** That is possible, but then you are paying for something you do not use.

We mention it because that is not a given with other services. There are parties that put content on a platform of their own and take it offline when you cancel. We do not consider that a fair way of working.

## How does NXEO differ from an ordinary findability tool?

There are many programs that measure your visibility. You get a dashboard with charts and a list of recommendations. After that you have to do it yourself.

The difference with NXEO is that the work also gets done. Measuring is the first step, not the service. The service is that content is added to your website every week, without you putting time into it.
