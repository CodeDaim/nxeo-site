Your customer asks ChatGPT who to call. The answer names three companies. How do you make sure you are one of them?

That is the question behind "how do I get mentioned by AI". The honest answer: there is no button and no trick. There are seven steps that demonstrably raise the odds. This guide puts them in order and points to the page where we work each one out.

## First: how does AI choose who to mention?

An AI system does not keep a ranking. It writes a new answer for each question. For that it uses two things: what the model read during training, and what it finds on the internet at that moment.

You have little influence on the first. You do on the second. What can be read clearly about your company today can be in an answer a few weeks from now. How that works exactly is in [how ChatGPT decides which companies to mention](#art_hoe_chatgpt_bepaalt_welke_bedrijven_het_noemt).

## Step 1. Measure where you stand

Do not type in your own company name. A new customer does not know your name yet. Ask the question the way they ask it: who does this, in my area?

Ask at least ten questions and write down who is mentioned. One answer is chance, ten questions show a pattern. The method is in [is my company mentioned in AI](#art_mijn_bedrijf_genoemd_in_ai). If you would rather not do it yourself, the [free baseline check](#nulmeting) does it for you.

## Step 2. Collect your customers' questions

Customers do not give AI keywords, they ask whole questions. We sort them into five types: who does this, who is the best, what is the difference, who can do it fast and what does it cost.

The best source is you: your phone, your email and your quotes. Thirty good questions will keep you going for a long time. Read on in [which questions do customers ask AI](#art_welke_vragen_stellen_klanten_aan_ai).

## Step 3. Say literally what you do, for whom and where

The most common reason a company is missing: the website does not say it. "We take the worry away" is not information. "We fit dormers in Apeldoorn and the surrounding area" is.

Write in ordinary sentences what you do, for whom and in which area. Also say where you do not go. More in [being found locally in AI](#art_lokaal_gevonden_worden_in_ai) and in [why am I not in AI recommendations](#art_waarom_sta_ik_niet_in_de_aanbevelingen_van_ai).

## Step 4. Answer each question in its own place

An AI system does not quote a whole page, it quotes one paragraph. Make that paragraph easy to find:

- Put the question in the title.
- Give the answer in the first sentence.
- Do not hide text behind a click.

That also goes for the question what it costs. You do not have to give a fixed price, but explain what the price depends on. Examples are in [a frequently asked questions page AI can quote](#art_veelgestelde_vragen_pagina_die_ai_kan_citeren).

## Step 5. Make sure they can read your website

Good text does not help if a system cannot reach it. Check that your website is not blocking search engines by accident, and that you are in the search engines the AI systems lean on.

## Step 6. Let others confirm it

Your own website says what you think of yourself. A system looks for sources that confirm it: reviews with text, a listing with a trade association, an article in a trade magazine.

A few real mentions that fit your trade do more than a hundred bought ones. Read [why AI trusts others](#art_reviews_en_vermeldingen_waarom_ai_op_anderen_vertrouwt).

## Step 7. Measure again and keep it up

Count on four to eight weeks for the first shifts and on three months for a picture you can trust. Stopping after two weeks is stopping too early. See [how long does it take before AI mentions your company](#art_hoe_lang_duurt_het_voordat_ai_je_bedrijf_noemt).

After that, measure the same questions every month. That shows what works, and whether an answer about you is still right. If it is not, read [AI says something wrong about my company](#art_ai_zegt_iets_verkeerds_over_mijn_bedrijf).

## What does not work

- **Buying a place.** The answer itself is not for sale.
- **Tricks with hidden text.** Systems read what a visitor reads.
- **Twenty pages with only a different place name.** That is filler.
- **Writing something once and leaving it there.** Regularity counts.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I pay to be mentioned?

No. An answer is written based on what can be read about you. You can invest in that content.

### Does this also work for a small company?

Yes. For specific questions about a trade or a region there is often little good content. Whoever answers that question clearly has a head start.

### Do I have to do something different for each AI system?

The basis is the same for all systems: clear content on your own website. The differences are in which search engine a system uses. Those are in the pages per system.

### How do I know whether it works?

By asking the same questions again and counting. Start with a measurement of today, then you have something to compare with. That is what the [free baseline check](#nulmeting) is for.
