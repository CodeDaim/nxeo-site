Perplexity is smaller than ChatGPT, but for a business owner it is interesting for one reason: it shows where it gets its answer from. Every answer comes with the sources as numbered links.

That makes Perplexity the easiest system to learn from. You see not only who is mentioned, but also why.

## How Perplexity arrives at its answer

Perplexity calls itself an answer engine. For every question it searches the internet, reads a number of pages and writes an answer from them. The pages it used are listed.

The difference from an ordinary chatbot is that searching is not an extra. It happens for every question. What the model once learned therefore weighs less than what can be found now.

## What that means for you

Two things.

**New content can count quickly.** A page you publish today can be used as soon as Perplexity can find it.

**You can see how it works.** Ask a question you want to be mentioned for and open the sources. They show which pages shaped the answer. Often these are not homepages, but pages that answer exactly that one question.

## Test it in ten minutes

1. Ask five questions a customer of yours would ask. Do not use your own name.
2. Write down per question which companies are mentioned.
3. Open the sources. Is it the company's own website, a comparison site or an article?
4. See what that page has that you do not. Usually: a clear answer to exactly that question.

What you learn here largely applies to the other systems too. The step by step plan is in [how do you get mentioned by AI](#art_hoe_word_je_genoemd_door_ai).

## Can Perplexity read your website?

Perplexity has its own crawler, PerplexityBot. It reads pages so it can show them as a source. If your website blocks that crawler, Perplexity cannot use your pages.

So check your robots.txt. Some websites and security services block all AI crawlers at once. That also stops the crawlers you actually want to let in. If you have WordPress, look at the [checklist](#art_wordpress_controlelijst_vindbaar_voor_ai).

## What helps to be chosen as a source

- **One question per page.** A page that answers one question well is chosen sooner than a page that half says ten things.
- **The answer at the top.** Start with the answer, then explain it.
- **Facts that can be checked.** Prices, lead times, service area. Be concrete.
- **A date that is right.** Keep pages up to date and show when they were updated.

More on how to build such a page is in [a frequently asked questions page AI can quote](#art_veelgestelde_vragen_pagina_die_ai_kan_citeren).

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Perplexity important enough to spend time on?

As a goal in itself usually not. As a way to learn, yes. What you do to serve as a source in Perplexity also helps with the other systems.

### Why does Perplexity mention a comparison site and not my company?

Because that site answers the question and your website does not. Answer the question yourself, on its own page.
